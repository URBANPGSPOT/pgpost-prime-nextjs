Privacy Safeguards

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026. This Privacy Policy outlines how PGSPOT collects, uses, and safeguards your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless co-living experience, verify identities, and handle transactions, we collect the following personal information:

  • Identity Information: Full name, date of birth, emergency contact details, and government-issued ID proofs (such as Aadhaar, PAN card, or passport).
  • Contact Information: Phone number, email address, and permanent residential address.
  • Financial Information: Payment details, transaction records, and invoice histories.
  • Security Records: CCTV recordings in public areas of properties for resident safety.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To process bookings, generate tenancy agreements, and verify identities.
  • To manage billing, collect rent payments, and handle refund deposits.
  • To provide support services, send emergency alerts, and respond to query forms.
  • To enhance building security and safety monitoring via public area CCTV cameras.

3. Data Sharing & Security

Security first: We apply appropriate technical and organizational measures (such as encrypted databases and access control) to protect your personal data from unauthorized access or alteration.

We do not sell or lease your personal information. Data is only shared with trusted third parties to complete financial transactions (payment gateways) or when mandated by law enforcement and local government authorities for resident verification purposes.

4. Cookies and Web Technologies

Our website uses standard cookies and session tokens to preserve layout settings, keep you authenticated on administrative panels, and capture anonymous visitor metrics (such as Google Analytics) to improve loading performance and site layouts.

5. Your Legal Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to incorrect data, or request the deletion of your data once your tenancy agreement has concluded and all dues have been cleared.

For questions, data access requests, or deletion requests, please contact our privacy compliance team at hello@pgspot.in.

Terms & Conditions

Last updated: June 17, 2026. Please read these Terms and Conditions carefully before booking or residing at any PGSPOT premium co-living property.

1. Acceptance of Agreement

By completing a booking, signing a tenancy agreement, or occupying a room in any PGSPOT property, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions. These terms apply to all residents, guests, and visitors.

2. Booking, Rent, and Security Deposit

  • Booking Confirmation: A booking is confirmed only upon the receipt of the security deposit and the verification of valid government-issued ID proofs.
  • Monthly Rent: Monthly rent is payable in advance on or before the 5th day of every calendar month. Delayed payments will attract a late fee penalty as specified in your agreement.
  • Security Deposit: The security deposit is refundable upon move-out after deducting unpaid dues, utility charges, or damages beyond normal wear and tear.

3. Lock-in Period and Notice Period

To maintain community stability, PGSPOT enforces a minimum lock-in period of three (3) months for all premium accommodations.

A written notice of at least thirty (30) days is strictly required prior to vacating the premises. Failure to provide adequate notice will result in the forfeiture of the security deposit.

4. Code of Conduct and House Rules

Residents are expected to maintain high standards of cleanliness, respect personal spaces, and foster a friendly environment.

  • Noise Control: Quiet hours are observed from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM daily.
  • Guest Policy: Visitors are permitted in common areas during designated hours. Overnight guests require prior approval from the property manager and are subject to guest charges.
  • Zero Tolerance Policy: The possession or consumption of illegal substances, weapons, or engagement in hazardous activities is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate eviction.

5. Amenities, Housekeeping, and Utilities

High-speed Wi-Fi, daily housekeeping, and common area power backups are provided as part of the monthly subscription. While we strive for 100% uptime, PGSPOT is not liable for temporary service interruptions from external service providers but will work swiftly to resolve them.

6. Liability and Safety

Residents are solely responsible for their personal belongings. PGSPOT provides CCTV surveillance and security guards, but accepts no liability for any loss, theft, or damage to personal items inside individual rooms.

For any clarifications regarding these Terms and Conditions, please contact us at hello@pgspot.in or speak directly to your Property Manager.
